Tandem

Privacy

Tandem (until 2.0.0: BeamNG Friends Launcher) has no account, no analytics and no ads. Everything you do in a session (driving, mods, chat, voice) goes straight between the host's PC and the friends who join. Since 2.0.0 there is one small service run by the developer, the session list: it only helps players find public sessions and passes a friend's reply to the host. What it sees is listed below.

What goes between you and your friends

When you host or join, these go directly between the PCs in that session:

WhatWho gets itWhy
Your internet address (IP)The host, and friends you share mods withNeeded for any direct connection; anyone you play with online can see it
Your player nameEveryone in the sessionShown in the player list, in chat and above your car
Your car, its parts, paint and position, damage, gear, trailerEveryone in the sessionSo friends see your car as you drive it
Chat messagesEveryone in the sessionNot saved anywhere after the session
Your voice, only while voice chat is on and you talkEveryone in the session, through the hostPlayed live, never recorded or saved. Turn voice chat off in Settings and the microphone is never opened
A random identity key made on your PC (public half only)The host you ask to joinSo a host can remember you and let you in without asking. It is not linked to your name, Windows account or PC
The host's map and shared modsFriends who join that hostThey download them from the host (and from friends already in the session)
Your download progressThe hostShown on the host's page
A reply link, only if the host's router needs one for a direct linkThe host, through whatever chat you send it inIt carries your public address and a port, signed so it can't be changed, and expires after 15 minutes

The connection between PCs is encrypted, and each invite is checked against the host's own certificate, so someone in the middle can't read or change it.

The reach check: when you host over the internet, the service knocks once on your own session port to see whether friends can reach you; it only ever knocks on the address that asked. Joining: the list is fetched; a reply you hand in holds the addresses your router gave you, as it does when you send it yourself. The service keeps nothing about a session after it ends and writes no logs of its own; the one exception is a session the developer took off the public list, whose internet address is remembered for one day so it cannot list again. Cloudflare, which runs it, handles the traffic as it does for any website; its dashboard shows the developer the internet address and country of recent requests, and Tandem copies them nowhere. On your home network, the launcher answers "is anyone hosting?" questions from other PCs on the same Wi-Fi with the session name, map, host name and player count, so friends at home can find it. It never sends the invite that way. Turn this off in Settings with Let people on my Wi-Fi find my session.

What stays on your PC

Get mods (when you switch it on) looks at the names and sizes of new files in your Downloads folder and opens only ZIP files, to see whether they are mods. This happens on your PC; nothing about your downloads is sent anywhere. A mod site you open is opened in your own browser, as if you had typed its address.

In the launcher's folder in your Windows user profile (%APPDATA%\beamng-friends-launcher):

  • your settings, including your player name and saved session setups;
  • recent sessions you joined (name, map and invite, so you can rejoin; invites expire);
  • your identity key and the friends you chose to let in automatically;
  • which developer keys the launcher trusts (only changed by a switch the developer signed);
  • downloaded mods waiting to be used, checked by fingerprint before use.

Nothing from that folder is sent anywhere, except what the tables above list. Copy report in Settings copies a problem report to your clipboard only when you press it. It has no invites, passwords or keys, but it does list folder paths and addresses, so read it before sending it to anyone.

In the game

The launcher adds a small script to your BeamNG user folder while you play together. It only talks to the launcher on your own PC (address 127.0.0.1, with a one-time password), never to the internet. It is removed again when the session ends.

The website

The website (tandembeamng.com) has no cookies, no analytics and no ads. It asks GitHub for the newest version (for the download button) and Tandem's own service for the public sessions open right now (a name, the game, the map and the number of players; never an address). Your browser remembers your language and the newest version for half an hour, only on your device. GitHub, which hosts the website, and Cloudflare, which runs its address, handle the traffic as they do for any website.
