Tandem

Terms of use

These are the plain rules for using Tandem (the app, its website tandembeamng.com, its online service and its Discord bot). By using Tandem you agree to them.

What Tandem is

Tandem is a free app made by one person, mhmd8p. It lets friends play BeamNG.drive, Assetto Corsa and a few other games together: one player hosts on their own PC and the others join. It is given to you as it is, without any promise that it will always work, fit your needs or stay available.

Using the app

  • Download Tandem only from tandembeamng.com. A copy from anywhere else may be changed and is not supported.
  • You may use Tandem for free, alone or with friends. You may not copy it, sell it, re-upload it, change it or publish it under another name.
  • Tandem updates itself only with versions signed by its developer. Do not try to get around that check.

Games and mods

  • You need your own copy of every game you play. Tandem does not sell, unlock or give away games.
  • Content you paid for (DLC, paid mods) and the games' own files are never sent to other players.
  • Mods belong to the people who made them. Share only mods you are allowed to share. If a mod maker asks you not to share their mod, don't.
  • Tandem never changes a game's own files, except that Assetto Corsa mod cars and tracks are added to the game's content folder after you say yes. Tandem does not turn off any protection of a game.

How to behave

  • Public sessions, session names, chat and the Discord bot are seen by other people. Don't use them to insult, threaten, spam or share anything illegal.
  • A host can remove or block any player. The developer can take a session off the public list, and can block a PC from the online service, when these rules are broken.
  • Don't attack, overload or try to break into the online service, another player's PC or the app.

The online service and the Discord bot

  • The online service (play.tandembeamng.com) helps players find public sessions, passes replies to hosts and runs the Discord bot. It may change, slow down or stop at any time.
  • A Discord server's managers decide whether to add the bot, and can remove it at any time. The bot posts public sessions and new versions. It cannot read the messages in a server.
  • What the service and the app see about you is explained in the privacy page.
